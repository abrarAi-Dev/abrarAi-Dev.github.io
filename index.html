01 · ABOUT

About

Abrar Shariff Mohammed. Analyst first, technologist by training, focused on where business, data and AI meet.

I work where business, data and AI meet. As a Business Analyst at Eficens Systems, I evaluate and implement AI workflows, design prompts, test agentic systems, and translate open ended questions such as "can AI help here?" into clear requirements, dashboards and working products.

Before Charlotte, my path ran through UNICEF India, where I served as a staff member and Youth Representative to the Board and helped build the UReport chatbot for young people across India, and McDonald's in Chicago, as a Software Engineering Intern. That combination taught me to weigh the technology and the people using it equally.

I am now completing my MS in Business Analytics at Anderson University while applying to fully funded AI PhD programs for Fall 2027. My focus is applied AI that solves concrete business problems, explained in language any stakeholder can act on.
